# TMMT / AIXMOS Compliance Disclaimers

Last updated: 2026-06-03. Owner: founder. Review cadence: every 90 days or when CFPB/FTC guidance changes.

These blocks are the **canonical copy** for compliance language across the funnel. Use them verbatim — do not paraphrase. If you need a variant, add it here first.

> NOTE: I am not a lawyer. Have these reviewed by counsel before any large ad spend. Trade groups: NACSO (credit services), AFSA, RVIA (lease-to-own). Federal touchpoints: CFPB (UDAAP, CROA), FTC (Holder Rule, FCRA), state AGs (CROA carve-outs).

---

## 1. Credit guidance — primary disclaimer (long form)

Place at footer of every credit-related page (rental flow, $97 product, credit education modules, email signatures of credit-program reps).

> **Not credit repair.** TMMT and AIXMOS provide educational information about consumer credit, score factors, and lender criteria. We do **not** represent ourselves to be, and are not, a credit repair organization as defined under the Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA, 15 U.S.C. § 1679a). We do not promise to remove, dispute, or alter accurate items on your credit report. We do not act as your agent in communications with credit bureaus, lenders, or collectors. Any actions you take on your credit profile are taken by you, with information we provide. Results vary; we make no guarantee that any specific action will raise your score by a specific amount or within a specific timeframe.

## 2. Credit guidance — short form (1 line)

For footer on TikTok bio link, IG bio, ad creative captions, SMS replies, anywhere space is tight.

> Educational only. Not credit repair. No score guarantee. See [domain]/legal/credit.

## 3. Vehicle rental / lease-to-own — Holder Rule + acceleration

For rental agreement, $97 enrollment receipt, LTO contract:

> This is a rental agreement and **not** an extension of credit or a retail installment sale at the point of execution. If at any time payment terms are restructured into a lease-to-own (LTO) or financed sale, you will be re-disclosed and asked to consent to a new agreement. Until then, no credit is being extended and no credit reporting will occur on your behalf without your separate written consent.

## 4. "Funding" referrals — broker / finder language

For any place where TMMT/AIXMOS introduces a customer to a third-party lender, factor, or funding source:

> TMMT/AIXMOS is **not** a lender. We do not underwrite, fund, service, or collect on loans or credit products. We may introduce you to independent third-party lenders or funding sources. If you elect to apply with any third-party source we introduce, you will contract directly with them, on their terms, and any decision to extend credit is theirs alone. We may receive a referral or marketing fee from third-party sources you choose to engage with. This fee does not change your cost or rate.

## 5. AI assistant / chatbot disclaimer

Add to system prompt of every customer-facing agent (rental bot, credit education bot, intake bot):

> This assistant uses AI to draft responses. It is not a lawyer, financial advisor, or human representative. For binding decisions, request escalation to a human team member at hello@<domain> or via in-app chat.

## 6. Background-check / fitness-to-rent disclaimer

Shown at the top of any background-check intake form:

> By submitting this form you authorize a one-time background check for the purpose of evaluating eligibility for vehicle rental, lease-to-own, or program enrollment. We retain the result for our recordkeeping requirements. Adverse decisions will be communicated with the source and basis as required by the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681). You may request a copy of the report we obtain on you within 60 days of any adverse decision at no cost.

## 7. SMS consent — TCPA / 10DLC

For lead capture forms that text follow-ups:

> By providing your phone number you consent to receive transactional and marketing text messages from TMMT/AIXMOS at the number provided, including messages sent by automatic means. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Frequency varies. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Privacy: [domain]/legal/privacy. Terms: [domain]/legal/sms.

## 8. Earnings / results disclaimer

For any page showing customer outcomes ("got approved", "raised score 80 pts", "funded $50k"):

> Customer-reported results. Individual results vary based on starting credit profile, income, debt, market conditions, and customer execution. Past results do not guarantee future results. No specific outcome is promised.
