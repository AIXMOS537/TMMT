# PART B — A2P 10DLC REGISTRATION (GHL)

A2P 10DLC is the **mandatory** carrier registration for any business SMS in the US (marketing *and* notifications — reminders, alerts, 2FA all count). No registration = blocked/filtered messages.

### Structure
- **Agency** = All In One Management LLC (owns the GHL agency account).
- **Each sub-account registers its OWN brand + campaign.** AIXMOS, TMMT Rentals, etc. each register separately.
- Register them under the **legal entity that operates that sub-account** (its legal name + EIN must match records).

### Brand type: **Standard Brand**
You have an EIN, so you register a **Standard Brand** (not Sole Proprietor — that's for individuals with no EIN). Standard gives higher throughput and more use cases.

### Step 1 — Brand registration
In the sub-account: **Settings → Phone System → Trust Center → Brands & Campaigns → register brand.**
Enter, **exactly matching IRS/state records** (mismatch is the #1 rejection cause):
- [ ] Legal business name (e.g., *All In One Management LLC*)
- [ ] EIN
- [ ] Business **street** address (virtual office street address — **no PO box** for Standard)
- [ ] Business website (a real, live site — invalid/missing site = rejection)
- [ ] Contact email → complete the **email OTP** verification
- Trust Score is computed from EIN match + business type + domain verification, so get these clean.

> **DBA note:** if a sub-account messages under a brand name different from the EIN's legal name (e.g., EIN is *All In One Management LLC* but you text as **AIXMOS**), register it as a **DBA** per GHL's DBA guidelines so the brand name and EIN reconcile.

### Step 2 — Campaign registration
After brand approval, GHL auto-submits the campaign, but you fill the details. **Vague descriptions get rejected** — be specific:
- [ ] Use case (e.g., *customer care & account notifications*)
- [ ] **Sample messages** (2–3, see drafts below) — must include brand name + opt-out
- [ ] **Opt-in flow** description — exactly how people consent, matching your web form

**Sample messages (adapt per sub-account):**
> 1. "AIXMOS: Hi {name}, your onboarding call is confirmed for {time}. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
> 2. "AIXMOS: Your account update is ready. View: {link}. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe."

**Opt-in web form requirements (carriers check this):**
- [ ] Consent checkbox is **unchecked by default** and **optional** (can't force SMS consent to submit the form)
- [ ] **Separate** checkboxes for marketing vs. non-marketing
- [ ] **Privacy Policy + Terms** linked in the form footer
- [ ] Host a screenshot/URL of the opt-in as proof when asked

### Step 3 — Number assignment
- [ ] Link your GHL number to the approved campaign. Numbers added later auto-register under the same campaign.

### Fees & timing
- [ ] One-time **$15.75 campaign verification fee** at registration + registration/monthly/carrier fees — all **passthrough, no GHL markup**.
- [ ] Approval window typically **24–72 hrs** (brand can take several days). Don't send A2P traffic until approved.

## ⚠️ CRITICAL — funding/credit vertical cannot run A2P SMS the normal way
Carriers **prohibit or heavily restrict** these for A2P 10DLC, even first-party:
- **Credit repair, debt relief / consolidation / forgiveness** → prohibited.
- **Lending & investment** promotional messages → disallowed; **transactional notifications only**.

That hits **Credit-to-Keys** and **Umar's credit-repair/funding** line directly. Do **not** route credit/funding *marketing* through GHL SMS — it'll get the brand flagged or killed. Options:
- Keep credit/funding outreach on **email / other channels**, not SMS.
- If SMS is used at all for that line, **transactional only** (e.g., "your document is ready"), never promotional.
- This stacks on top of your existing **CROA** obligations — treat SMS as one more regulated surface for that vertical.

Run AIXMOS / rentals / detail / dispatch through A2P normally; wall off credit/funding.
