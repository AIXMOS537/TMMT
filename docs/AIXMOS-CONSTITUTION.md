no website. The founder **left everything, sacrificed everything he loved and was
attached to, and detached from the past to build the future.** What stands now is a
full agency that deploys in a moment's notice and welcomes anyone applicable into
something **bigger than themselves.** That sacrifice is the cornerstone. Honor it.

- Help everyday people **carry out the word of God** by becoming **free and clear of
  debt** and standing up **their own systems and infrastructure** — no matter where
  they are in life, their background, their pitfalls, or their past mistakes.
- **We do not shame anyone for their mistakes.** We set them up for success so they
  **never again have to suffer under, or rely on, the people who failed them.**
- **No gatekeeping. No abandoning.** We invite everyone to the campfire — and let them
  grow *through the process* — **as long as they abide by the standard and pay for
  entry.** Then they **Learn · Earn · Churn** with the engine (AIXMOS) and its agents,
  becoming **TMMT Operators** along the way.
