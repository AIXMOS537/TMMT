## Engagement terms (how a deal starts)

- **50% deposit = go.** A half-down payment proves the client is **100% serious**
  and **triggers the backend dev + research team** to begin discovery.
- **What the deposit buys immediately:** discovery — the team works to understand
  exactly what the client wants and their **end goal**, then relays clean,
  structured requirements **across the network** (TMMT operators + AIXMOS agents).
- **Balance** due per milestone / on delivery (define in the engagement letter).
- **$50K upfront** (or as close to 50% of project as possible) gets the fastest
  start and the full horizontal + vertical build.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **Public only ever sees AIXMOS.** Project X / HAILMARY stays behind the curtain.
- **Deposits + scope in writing** (engagement letter) before discovery — protects
  both sides.
- **Remote access is consented + logged.** Lead-backend partnership, never covert.
- **Honest claims.** Sell the system + the build + the management. The e-commerce
  engine earns when fed traffic and kept on — never promise guaranteed income.
- **Credit = "guidance," never "repair."** Affiliate pays on real sales only.
- **People first** — founders and operators get training (Operator Academy) + the
  `compass`. Protect them.
