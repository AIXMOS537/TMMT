# AIXMOS Affiliate Agreement — Draft v1

> ⚠️ **Lawyer review required before any affiliate signs this.** This is a working draft to anchor the conversation with counsel, not final legal copy. It captures the business terms in plain English so a Virginia (or your jurisdiction) attorney can convert it into binding language.

---

## Parties

This Affiliate Agreement (the "Agreement") is between:

- **AIXMOS** — All In One Management Solutions, LLC (the "Company"), a [Virginia / DE / state] limited liability company
- **You** — the individual or business entity named in the affiliate application (the "Affiliate")

Effective Date: the date the Company approves your affiliate application.

---

## 1. What the Affiliate does

The Affiliate promotes AIXMOS's $97 membership product to their audience using their unique referral link. The Affiliate is not an employee, agent, or representative of the Company. The Affiliate is an independent contractor for compensation purposes only.

The Affiliate may not:
- Make claims on behalf of AIXMOS that AIXMOS itself does not make
- Use the AIXMOS name, logo, or brand outside of approved marketing assets in the Starter Kit
- Sell, sublicense, or transfer their referral link
- Bid on AIXMOS branded keywords in paid search
- Run paid social ads using the AIXMOS name without prior written approval

---

## 2. Compensation

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Rate | $35 per qualified $97 sale attributed to the Affiliate's referral link |
| Tier-up | Affiliates with 10+ sales in a calendar month qualify for a rate of $45-$50 per sale the following month, at the Company's discretion |
| Payment cadence | Monthly, on the 1st of the month following the sale month |
| Payment method | Stripe Connect (preferred) or PayPal |
| Refund clawback | If a member refunds within 14 days, the corresponding commission is reversed |
| Tax form | Affiliates earning $600+ in a calendar year will receive a 1099-NEC |

A "qualified sale" means:
- A new customer (not a previous AIXMOS member)
- Who clicked the Affiliate's unique referral link within the 60-day attribution window
- Who completed payment for the $97 AIXMOS membership
- Whose payment has cleared and has not been refunded inside 14 days

---

## 3. Compliance — non-negotiable

The Affiliate MUST NOT use, in any post, video, ad, DM, email, call, or other communication promoting AIXMOS:

- "Guarantee" or "guaranteed"
- "Approved" or "you will be approved"
- "Fund" or "funded" or "we will fund you"
- "Fix your credit" or "credit repair" or "remove negative items"
- "100%" or "no risk" or "I promise"

The Affiliate MUST use AIXMOS-approved language: coach, guide, plan, help, work with, may be able to.

These restrictions exist to protect both parties from Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA) and FTC liability. Violation triggers:
- **First violation:** written warning, immediate takedown of offending content
- **Second violation inside 30 days:** termination of the Affiliate Agreement, forfeiture of unpaid commissions

The Company has sole discretion in determining whether a communication violates this Section.

---

## 4. Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason with 30 days written notice. The Company may terminate immediately upon a compliance violation under Section 3.

Upon termination:
- The Affiliate's unique referral link is deactivated
- Commissions earned but not yet paid will be paid on the next regular payout date
- The Affiliate must remove AIXMOS branded content from their owned channels within 14 days

---

## 5. Ownership and confidentiality

- AIXMOS owns its name, logo, marketing assets, customer data, and all materials in the Starter Kit
- The Affiliate is granted a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license to use those assets solely to promote AIXMOS during the term of this Agreement
- The Affiliate may not share, resell, or repurpose AIXMOS materials for any other product or program
- Customer data generated through the Affiliate's referrals belongs to AIXMOS, not the Affiliate

---

## 6. Independent contractor relationship

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or franchise relationship. The Affiliate is solely responsible for:
- Their own taxes (income, self-employment, sales)
- Their own business expenses
- Their own insurance, licenses, and registrations
- Any sub-contractors or assistants they engage

---

## 7. Indemnification

The Affiliate agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Company against any claim, loss, or expense arising from:
- The Affiliate's violation of Section 3 (Compliance)
- The Affiliate's false or misleading representations about AIXMOS
- The Affiliate's violation of any law in their promotional activities

---

## 8. Governing law and dispute resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. Any dispute will be resolved by binding arbitration in [city, VA] under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

---

## 9. Acceptance

By submitting the AIXMOS Affiliate Application and clicking "I agree" on the application form, the Affiliate accepts this Agreement.
