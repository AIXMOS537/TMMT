## How to use it (4 baby steps)

1. **Turn it ON** → open the black box (Terminal) and type **`booyah`** → Enter.
2. **The magic words** (type one, it does the thing):
   - 🟢 `booyah` — turn everything ON
   - 🔵 `menu` — show the picture board
   - 🟣 `watchtower` — is everything OK?
   - 🩵 `compass` — feel calm, one good step
   - 🔴 `dark` — STOP everything NOW
   - 🟡 `light` — turn it back on (needs **your secret word**)
3. **The STOP button** → say `dark` and everything stops. Say `light` (your word)
   to start again. Only YOUR word works — nobody else's.
4. **Feel good** → say `compass`. It gives you one nice step. *Protect your peace.
   Walk toward God, one step at a time.*
