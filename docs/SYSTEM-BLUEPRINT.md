## 2. What it actually is — three layers

Think of it as one machine with three layers:

| Layer | Name | What it does | Who touches it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🧠 **Brain** | **AIXMOS** | AI agents + command desk that watch the numbers, prep the work, and keep everyone moving. The encoded playbook. | Owner (and the agents, automatically) |
| ⚙️ **Engine** | **TMMT OS** | The operations platform — fleet, customers, payments, tickets, dispatch, reporting. Where the business is actually run. | Owner + staff/operators |
| 📣 **Funnel** | **AIXMOS public funnel** | The front door — forms, offers, checkout. Turns strangers into paying customers across the verticals. | Customers + operators driving them |

Three deployed apps make this real (one ecosystem):
- **TMMT Ops** — day-to-day operations.
- **Command Center** — the owner/leadership cockpit (revenue, dispatch, the brain).
- **AIXMOS Landing** — the public funnel (`/kits`, `/build`, the forms).

## 3. The value ladder — the verticals everyone climbs

Every person enters somewhere and can climb. This is the whole offer in one
picture:

```
   START                                                          OWN IT
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────►
   🚗 Rental   →   💳 $97/mo Membership   →   📈 Credit Guidance   →   🏗️ Build / Kit
   get moving      the recurring entry        the upsell               their own system
                   (AIXMOS access)            ("guidance," not          $97–$2,997 kits
                                               "repair")                 $3,750–$50k builds
```

- **🚗 Rental** — the front door. Get them in a vehicle, earning.
- **💳 $97/mo AIXMOS Membership** — recurring access to the systems + coaching.
- **📈 Credit Guidance** — the bridge from renting to qualifying (compliance
  vocabulary is non-negotiable — "guidance," never "repair").
- **🏗️ Builds & Kits** — for the ones who want to run their own:
  **kits** ($97 → $2,997) and **done-for-you builds** ($3,750 → $50,000).
- **🤝 Affiliate** — anyone can refer and get paid on collected sales.

Same ladder, different verticals (rental, business systems, e-commerce,
funding) — pick the rung that fits the person in front of you.

## 4. Who plugs in where

| Audience | What they get | What they do | What they DON'T get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner (you)** | The whole machine + the brain | Set direction, the agents run ops | — |
| **Operators / sales** | A hosted login + 3 bookmarks | Funnel people up the ladder, get paid | The code, secrets, or agents |
| **Dealers** | Their own deployed kit/build | Run their lot on your system | Your IP — they license a deployed copy |
| **Partners / investors** | Read-only portal | See their vehicles' status (own cars only) | Customer data, contracts, payments |
| **Customers / members** | The offers + the `/learn` path | Rent → join → get guidance → build | Anything operational |

**The rule that protects everything:** people get **access to use**, never
**the keys to own**. Operators sign into a hosted app; they never receive the
repository, the `.env`, the API keys, or the AI agents. The machine is the moat.

## 5. How the money moves

```
 Operator drives a lead  →  Public funnel / GHL checkout  →  Payment captured
        (gets credit)              (the offer)                     │
                                                                   ▼
                              Recorded automatically in TMMT  →  Owner sees it on /revenue
                                                                   │
                              Affiliate commission on collected sales  →  /affiliates payout
```

- Payments run through hosted checkout (Stripe via GHL) — **no one handles your
  keys**.
- Every sale lands in the ledger automatically; the owner watches it live on
  **`/revenue`** (collected, recurring, outstanding, by product, by month).
- Referrers are credited and paid on **collected** sales via **`/affiliates`**
  (never on money you haven't received).
