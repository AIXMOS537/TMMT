# AIXMOS Setter Kit — for Justin (Philippines, first operator)

> Your role: **appointment-setter + VA for AIXMOS (All-In-One Management Solution).**
> You **book qualified calls** for everyone who wants what the owner offers, you
> **learn** the business, and you **earn**. You act on the owner's behalf — within
> your scope, confidential always. The owner closes the big deals; you line them up.

Welcome to the movement, Justin. One step at a time. 🚀

---

## 1. Your one job: LEARN · EARN · CHURN
- **LEARN** — study the offers below + the scripts. Shadow the owner. Know the value.
- **EARN** — you get paid for **qualified, booked, showed-up appointments** (and on
  closes). The owner sets your commission/terms.
- **CHURN** — volume. Consistent daily outreach → booked calls → repeat. Show up daily,
  the numbers follow.

## 2. What you're booking appointments for (the offer ladder)
You set appointments for **any price point** — meet people where they are, then move
them up:

| Tier | Offer | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Starter services / consults (owner's price ladder) | quick yes → book a call |
| Build | Custom builds / management setup | qualify → book a discovery call |
| **Flagship** | **$50K — Full AIXMOS stack + builds** | qualify hard → book the owner directly |
| **Coming soon** | **$25K — Done-For-You E-commerce setup** | capture interest → waitlist + book |

> **You SET, the owner CLOSES the high-ticket.** Your win = a qualified, confirmed,
> show-up appointment on the owner's calendar. Pricing + final deals = owner only.

## 3. Your daily flow (simple)
1. **Reach out / receive leads** (inbound from ads, or outbound DMs/messages).
2. **Qualify** — do they want to grow or run a business better? Budget/fit?
3. **Book** a discovery call on the owner's calendar.
4. **Confirm + remind** (day before + day of) so they show up.
5. **Log** the lead + outcome in the CRM (owner sets you up with scoped access).

## 4. Your scripts (truthful, booking-focused — never overpromise)
**Opener (DM/message):**
> "Hey [name] — saw you're into [growing your business / ecommerce]. We help people
> run an all-in-one setup with AI + a real team so it runs without you babysitting it.
> Open to a quick free call to see if it fits? No pressure."

**Qualify:**
> "What are you trying to set up or grow right now?" · "Are you the decision-maker?" ·
> "If it's a fit, are you ready to move in the next few weeks?"

**Book:**
> "Perfect — let's get you 15 min with the owner who'll walk you through exactly how
> it'd work for you. Does [day/time] work?"

**Reminder:**
> "Looking forward to your call with [owner] tomorrow at [time] — here's the link. Reply
> CONFIRM so we hold your spot."

**Price objection (don't sell price — sell the call):**
> "Totally fair — that's exactly what the call is for. The owner shows you the ROI and
> the options at every level. Worst case you leave with a plan. Want me to lock the time?"

> ❌ Never say "guaranteed income/results", never invent prices, never promise what the
> owner hasn't approved. Truth sells and keeps us clean.

## 5. The rules (keep yourself + the owner safe)
- **Truthful always.** No guarantees, no fake claims. Book the call; the owner delivers value.
- **Confidential always.** Never share how/why anything is built (you agreed at onboarding).
- **Scoped access.** You see only your assigned leads + the booking calendar — not
  everything (that's the owner's data-access rule, it protects clients *and* you).
- **Owner approves** pricing, deals, and anything that goes out under the company name.
- You **act on the owner's behalf** to set appointments — that authority is granted,
  scoped, and logged.

- The owner will connect you to the **calendar + CRM** (scoped) so your booked calls land.
