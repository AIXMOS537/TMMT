# Red Hood's Operator Kit — Credit · Funding · Lead-Gen

> For **X Umar / Red Hood**, operator. Your lane: credit repair, business
> funding, and lead generation via SaaS. This is your starter kit. You're in **DEV**
> — you can't break anything. One step at a time.

## Your 1 job right now
**Onboard more clients + run ads to close and secure leads.** That's it. Everything
below helps you do that safely and fast.

## Your daily flow
1. `bash scripts/whereami.sh` — confirm you're in DEV (green). Always know where you are.
2. Write your ad / sales message.
3. **Check it's safe** before posting: `bash scripts/check-my-ad.sh my-ad.txt`
   - 🟩 SAFE TO POST → go. 🟥 DO NOT POST → fix the flagged words, check again.
4. Use the ready templates in `content/consumer-facing/redhood-ad-templates.md`.
5. Log leads into the pipeline (GHL) — your owner sets you up with scoped access.

## The rules of your lane (keep yourself + the owner safe)
Credit + funding is regulated (CROA / FTC). The gate auto-blocks the danger words,
but know them:
- ❌ NEVER: "guaranteed deletion", "raise score by X points", "CPN/new credit
  identity", "erase bad credit", or charging before the work is done.
- ✅ ALWAYS: educate, help dispute **inaccurate** items, free consult, and include
  the disclosures (results vary, not legal/financial advice).
- ⚖️ Before charging anyone: the owner clears the legal pre-sale gate
  (`docs/CREDIT-FUNDING-COMPLIANCE.md`). You close; the owner green-lights billing.

## What you can and can't touch
- ✅ You: your DEV sandbox, your ad copy, your leads, the templates + checker.
- ❌ Not yours: production, other people's data, the architecture/how-it's-built
  (you agreed to keep everything confidential), raw keys (secrets are brokered).
- Owner approval is needed for anything that goes live or out to a real customer.
