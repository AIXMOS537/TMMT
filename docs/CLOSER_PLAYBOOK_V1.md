# Closer & Setter Playbook — V1

**Owner:** Justin (AIXMOS side) + Dominique (TMMT side)
**Roles covered:** Bibbs (TMMT Closer), Sumaima (AIXMOS Closer), Areesha (Renewals + Care), Rida (TMMT Setter)
**Goal:** Make the in-house team genuinely elite. KPI-driven, script-disciplined, compliance-locked. Measured weekly. No drift.

---

## 1. What good looks like (one rubric, scored every call review)

Each reviewed call gets scored 1–5 on each dimension. Anything under 3 triggers a coaching note. Three under-3s in a month = put on a 30-day plan.

| Dimension | 5 (elite) | 3 (passable) | 1 (fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compliance** | Zero banned words, used "coach/plan/help" naturally | One slip, self-corrected | Used "guarantee/approve/fix/repair/promise" |
| **Hook** (first 15 sec) | Named the lead's situation, got a "yes I'm still interested" inside 15 sec | Got there in 30-45 sec | Lost them inside 30 sec |
| **Discovery** | Asked 3+ qualifying questions before pitching | Asked 1-2 | Skipped straight to price |
| **Pitch fit** | Tied product to a specific thing the lead said | Generic pitch | Pitched something the lead didn't ask for |
| **Objection handling** | Calm, addressed the real concern, didn't argue | Got flustered but recovered | Argued or got hostile |
| **Close** | Asked for the close directly, used silence after | Soft-asked | Never asked |
| **Next step** | Got payment OR a firm scheduled callback with calendar invite sent | Vague follow-up promised | No next step set |

**Calls are scored by Justin (AIXMOS) and Dominique (TMMT), 1 call per closer per week.** Score is shown publicly in #sales Slack channel. Public scoreboard = peer pressure = improvement.

## 3. Top 8 objections — compliance-safe scripts

### TMMT closer (Bibbs) — rentals

**1. "Do you check credit?"**
> "We don't pull credit. We need a valid driver's license, deposit, and the first week's payment. That's it. Easier than a dealership for most of our customers."

**2. "Why is the weekly rate higher than a regular rental?"**
> "Because we're long-term. We take a deposit once and lock you in week after week — no daily-rate surprises, no credit check, no being told no. For most folks who can't easily get a car the other way, the math works out fast."

**3. "Can I do it without a deposit today?"**
> "I get it. To hold the car I need the deposit today — otherwise it goes to the next caller. Can you do half now and half on pickup tomorrow?"

**4. "I'll call you back."**
> "Totally fair. Just so you're not let down — the car's not held without the deposit. If it's gone tomorrow, I'd hate for that to be the reason. Anything I can answer right now so you can decide?"

### AIXMOS closer (Sumaima) — $97 membership

**1. "What exactly do I get for $97?"**
> "A real coach reviewing your money and credit situation, and a written plan within 48 hours showing your next moves. Not a generic PDF — a plan made for you. Most members say it's the best $97 they've spent because they finally know what to do next."

**2. "Can you guarantee my score will go up?"**
> "No — and any company that does is lying to you, which is why we don't. What we guarantee is the plan and the coaching. The results come from doing the work in the plan."

**3. "I tried something like this before and it didn't work."**
> "Yeah, most credit-repair pitches over-promise and under-deliver. We're not credit repair — we're coaching. You'll know within 48 hours whether the plan makes sense for you. If it doesn't, you tell us and we refund. That fair?"

**4. "I don't have $97 right now."**
> "I hear you. The way we look at it — if $97 is the thing standing between you and a plan for the next 12 months of your money, we'd rather you put it on a card and start. Most members make that back inside 30 days just from one thing the plan tells them to stop doing."

## 5. Lead triage rule (stop wasting closer time)

Leads must be tagged before they hit a closer. Setter (Rida for TMMT, Sumaima for cold AIXMOS) handles this gate.

| Tag | Goes to | Time-to-touch |
|---|---|---|
| `hot-ready-now` | Closer (Bibbs/Sumaima) | < 5 min |
| `warm-this-week` | Closer, scheduled callback | < 30 min |
| `cold-needs-nurture` | GHL automation, no closer time | Auto |
| `unqualified` | Disqualified, archived | N/A |

**The unqualified tag is the most important one.** Currently the team treats every lead as hot, burns closer time on tire-kickers, and lets actually-hot leads cool off. Hard rule: no qualifying questions answered = cold or unqualified. Closers only see hot or warm.

## 6. Compliance trip-wires (auto-fail any call)

If any reviewed call contains these words from the closer, the call is an automatic 1 on Compliance and triggers immediate Slack DM with the clip:

- "guarantee" / "guaranteed"
- "approve" / "approved" / "you'll be approved"
- "fund" / "funded" / "we'll fund you"
- "fix your credit" / "credit repair" / "remove items"
- "100%" / "no risk" / "I promise"

Three trip-wires in 30 days = pulled off phones until retrained.

## 7. Role-play — every Tuesday 2pm (30 min)

Pairs swap weekly. One person plays a tough lead, other person closes. Then switch. Justin or Dominique listens to one pair per week and gives feedback.

**Pair rotation:**
- Week 1: Bibbs ↔ Sumaima
- Week 2: Sumaima ↔ Areesha
- Week 3: Bibbs ↔ Rida
- Week 4: Areesha ↔ Rida
- Repeat

**Tough-lead scripts (rotate weekly):**
1. The skeptic: "Sounds like a scam, prove it."
2. The price-anchor: "AAA Rentals is $200 a week, why are you $300?" / "Why $97 when others are $49?"
3. The promise-seeker: "Just tell me — will you fix my credit, yes or no?"
4. The time-waster: "Just tell me everything, I'm not ready to decide today."

The closer's job is to handle without breaking compliance OR losing the lead.

## 9. Hiring trigger (when to add another closer)

Do not hire when "we're busy." Hire when:
- Two consecutive weeks where >20% of `hot-ready-now` leads go untouched for >2 hours
- OR Sumaima/Bibbs has 5+ days/week of hitting their daily floor for 3 weeks straight (they're full)

Otherwise: tune the existing team. New hires before the playbook is dialed = chaos doubled.
