# FIT FOR THE MISSION — Gatekeeping Doctrine
**Authority:** PROJECT X HAILMARY · **Updated:** 2026-07-06

> For the people. Low overhead after setup. Not everyone gets in — and that's justice, not cruelty.

---

## Why we test

Keeping unfit help hurts everyone — the operator, the network, and the people we serve. TMMT is meant to **make money with low overhead** after initial payment and setup. Backend management runs through **executives you keep** — not an unlimited headcount.

---

## Who must pass

| Applicant | Fit test | Executive review |
|-----------|----------|------------------|
| **New operator** | Required (80%+) | No — auto if pass |
| **Returning team member** | Required | **Yes — always** |
| **Failed / past operator** | Required | **Yes — always** |
| **New partner / vendor** | Required | Case by case |

**Old team does NOT get automatic comeback.** They pass the test like everyone else. Executives decide if they're fit.

## What we test (6 questions)

1. Mission alignment — for the people, not extraction  
2. Low overhead — share links, AI qualifies, earn split  
3. Integrity — no false credit promises, protect owner/family  
4. Honesty — accountability if they failed before  
5. Human dignity — every lead is a person  
6. Growth — Academy, tracked links, earn before asking more  

---

## Executive backend (your team)

Operators get **scoped access** — links, Academy, earnings.  
**Executives** handle backend management, reviews, escalations.  
**You** on Carry — private matters, approvals, kill-switch.

Not doing justice = letting unfit people in.  
Doing justice = test → fit → earn → churn.
