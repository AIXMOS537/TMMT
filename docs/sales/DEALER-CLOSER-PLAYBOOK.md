# Dealer Closer Playbook — Mom & Pop (15-min demo + close)

**Phase 1 only:** Software kit ($997–$3,497 + monthly). No credit recovery until legal gates clear.

**Primary offer:** Dealer Bundle — $3,497 setup + $697/mo

---

## Before the call (2 min prep)

- [ ] Pull their `/forms/dealer-apply` submission or GHL contact
- [ ] Confirm: independent dealer, 25+ units/mo, decision-maker on call
- [ ] Open demo tabs: `/kits`, `/forms/lead-intake`, Command Center (your instance)
- [ ] Have GHL checkout link ready (Dealer Bundle)

---

## The 15-minute demo flow

### Min 0–2: Hook

> "Thanks for jumping on. Quick question — where do your floor leads go today when someone walks in or calls? Spreadsheet, texts, or your DMS?"

*Listen. Mirror their pain.*

> "What I'm going to show you is your **own** system — not a shared login. Your customers, your fleet, your numbers. Takes about 15 minutes."

### Min 2–7: Demo TMMT Ops

1. Show `/forms/lead-intake` — "Every inquiry lands here automatically"
2. Show admin dashboard — customers, vehicles, payments, tickets
3. Show one ticket flow — "Customer has a flat tire at 9pm — your team sees it instantly"

**Say:** "This is what your floor runs on. No credit check on rentals — different from your F&I lane."

### Min 7–10: Demo Command Center (Dealer Bundle only)

1. Owner view — portfolio, KPIs, revenue trend
2. Role portals — "Owner sees everything, floor manager sees ops only"

**Say:** "You stop asking 'how'd we do this week?' — you open one screen."

### Min 10–12: ROI napkin

Write on screen or paper:

| | |
|---|---|
| Extra 1–2 deals/mo from captured leads | $2,500–$5,000 gross |
| Dealer Bundle cost | $697/mo |
| Setup (one-time) | $3,497 |

> "If this helps you close **one** deal you would've lost in the shuffle, it pays for itself every month."

### Min 12–15: Close

> "Setup is $3,497, then $697 a month. We deploy your own instance in 48–72 hours, train your team, and you're live in two weeks. I can send the checkout link right now — want Ops only at $997, or the full Dealer Bundle?"

**Silence. Wait.**

## Tier selection guide

| Signal | Recommend |
|--------|-----------|
| Owner on call, wants full visibility | **Dealer Bundle** |
| Floor manager only, owner not involved | **Ops Kit** — loop owner before close |
| "We just need lead capture" | **Ops Kit** |
| Multi-location same owner | **Dealer Bundle** + note multi-location in contract |
| Wants credit recovery first | **Pass or defer** — software first, recovery Phase 3 |

## Objection scripts (dealer-specific)

**"We already have a DMS / DealerSocket / etc."**
> "Perfect — TMMT isn't replacing your DMS. It's your rental/fleet ops layer and owner command center. Most dealers run both."

**"Can we just use your login?"**
> "Not for independent stores. Your customer list and financials stay on **your** instance — your keys, your backup. Same product, your house."

**"What about declined buyers / credit repair?"**
> "That's a separate lane we offer after you're live on ops. Today I'm showing you the floor system — clean leads, clean follow-up, owner visibility."

**"Too expensive for a 30-car lot."**
> "Ops Kit starts at $997 + $297/mo. That's less than one deal you lose because a lead fell through the cracks."

**"Send me info / I'll think about it."**
> "Absolutely. I'll email the one-pager. Fair warning — we hold one dealer slot per city. Want me to hold [City] for 7 days while you decide?"

**"Does this actually work?"**
> "Walk through the live demo you just saw — that's production, not a mockup. I'll connect you with [reference] after your go-live if you want a peer call."

## Compliance — never say

- "Guaranteed approval"
- "Credit repair"
- "We'll fix your credit"
- "We recover declined buyers" (Phase 3 only — not in Phase 1 close)

**Use instead:** "credit guidance," "lead capture," "owner visibility," "fleet ops"
