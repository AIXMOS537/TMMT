# Explain It & Demo It — the simple version

For showing **the Moe Legacy owner, his investors, and his team** how easy this
is. No tech words. Anyone can run the demo from this page.

---

## Part 1 — Explain it like I'm 5

> **It's a lemonade stand that runs itself — for car rentals and helping people
> own a business.**

- The **recipe book** is already written (that's the 7–10 years of work + Moe's
  standard). Nobody has to figure anything out.
- There's a **robot helper** (we call it AIXMOS) that watches everything and
  tells you what needs you today — like a manager that never sleeps.
- **Anyone can work the counter.** You log in on a website, click a few buttons.
  Nothing to install. Nothing you can break.
- **You can see every dollar from your phone**, the moment it comes in.
- People start small (rent a car), then grow (become a member, fix their
  footing, build their own thing). **We get paid when they move up. They win, we
  win.**

That's it. **A business in a box that you run from a website.**

### The picture (say it while drawing this)
```
   Stranger  →  fills a form  →  becomes a customer  →  climbs the ladder  →  $$$
                                                                          you see it live
```

## Part 2 — The 5-minute demo (anyone can run this)

**Before you start:** open the app URL, and have **one login** ready (ask the
owner — role doesn't matter for the tour). Have a phone or laptop. That's it.

Go in this order. Each step: **what to click** → **what to say.**

### 1. "Here's what we sell" (30 sec)
- **Click:** open **`/kits`** then **`/build`**.
- **Say:** "These are our offers — from a $97 starter all the way to a $50,000
  done-for-you business. Real prices, real checkout. This is the storefront."

### 2. "Here's a customer raising their hand" (45 sec)
- **Click:** open **`/forms/lead-intake`** → type a fake name + phone + email →
  **Submit**.
- **Say:** "That's how a customer comes in. They fill this out, hit send, and —"
  (a **Thank You** screen appears) "— they're captured automatically. No
  paperwork, no spreadsheet. The system already has them."

### 3. "Here's mission control" (60 sec)
- **Click:** go to **`/login`**, sign in → you land on the **Dashboard** (`/`).
- **Say:** "This is the cockpit. Up top, **'Your Mission Now'** tells you exactly
  what needs you today — overdue payments, new leads, open tickets — or says
  'all clear.' The robot did the watching. You just act."

### 4. "Here's the money — live" (60 sec)
- **Click:** open **`/revenue`**.
- **Say:** "Every dollar collected this month, what's recurring, what's still
  owed, and a chart of the last six months. This is the business's pulse, on one
  screen. From your phone, anywhere."

### 5. "Here's how helpers get paid" (45 sec)
- **Click:** open **`/affiliates`**.
- **Say:** "Anyone who refers a customer shows up here and gets paid — but only
  on sales that actually clear. So the people helping us are motivated, and it's
  all tracked automatically."

### 6. Close (30 sec)
- **Say:** "So: customers come in the front, the system does the paperwork, the
  robot tells us what to do, money shows up here, and helpers get paid. **Years
  of work, running itself, from a website.** That's the whole thing."

> **Demo rule:** keep moving, don't explain the plumbing. The point isn't *how*
> it works — it's *how easy* it is to run.

## Part 4 — Why it's *this* easy (the proof points)

- ✅ **Nothing to install** — it's a website. Phone, laptop, anything.
- ✅ **Can't break it** — everyone only sees their own buttons; the dangerous
  stuff is locked away.
- ✅ **The robot does the watching** — AIXMOS surfaces what needs attention so
  no one has to dig.
- ✅ **The paperwork is automatic** — leads, payments, and referrals record
  themselves.
- ✅ **You see the money instantly** — `/revenue` updates the moment a sale
  clears.
- ✅ **Already real** — live fleet, customers, and payments. Not a demo built
  for the pitch.
