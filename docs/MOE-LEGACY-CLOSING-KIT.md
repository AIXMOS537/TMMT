# Moe Legacy Closing Kit — taste → work leads → close → paid (self-serve)

> Built so **Umar's team closes on their own** — copy-paste scripts, the offer,
> objection handling, and the one-command close. The point: they make money
> without the Owner having to personally work every lead. Compliance is baked in
> (credit = **"guidance," never "repair"**). Companions: `scripts/moe-hot`
> (triage), `scripts/moe-brief` (daily), `scripts/new-deal.sh` (paperwork),
> `docs/OFFER-STACK.md` (prices), `docs/MOE-LEGACY-PORTAL.md`.

## The money flow (the whole thing on one line)
```
  LEAD captured → free TASTE (readiness score) → WORK it (moe-hot + scripts)
       → CLOSE the $97 + service → PAPERWORK (deal) → DEPOSIT = paid
```

## The offer (what Moe Legacy sells)
| Step | What | Price | Why they say yes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taste** | Free funding-readiness score + plan (the `/learn` portal) | **$0** | no-risk, shows them where they stand |
| **Entry** | Membership — guidance + funnels + the path | **$97/mo** | low bar, everyone starts here |
| **Core** | Credit-guidance program + funding readiness | per `OFFER-STACK` | the real work toward funding |
| **Funded** | Funding readiness → lender/Fund&Grow/Credit Suite hand-off | referral/fee | the payoff |

Sell the **system + the path**, never a guaranteed score or amount.

## The "taste" hook (what gets them in the door)
Send every lead to the **free readiness check** (the `/learn` intake → score). It's
the no-risk yes: they fill the funding intake, get a readiness ring + a plan, and
now they're a warm lead inside the portal. That's the taste that closes.

## Copy-paste scripts (compliant, ready to send)

**SMS — first touch (within 5 min of a lead):**
> Hey {{first}}, it's {{you}} with Moe Legacy 👋 Saw you're looking at funding.
> I can run you a **free readiness check** — shows exactly where you stand and the
> plan to get fundable. Takes 2 min, no cost. Want the link? (Reply STOP to opt out.)

**SMS — send the taste:**
> Here's your free readiness check: {{link}}. Fill it out and I'll walk you through
> your number + the plan on a quick call. When's good today?

**Call opener:**
> "Thanks for grabbing the readiness check. Real quick — my job is **guidance**:
> I show you what lenders look at and the steps to get fundable. No guarantees on
> scores or amounts, but a clear plan. Sound fair? Let's look at your number…"

**The close (after the plan):**
> "Here's how we work it together: you start on the **$97/month** membership, we
> open your guidance plan, and you climb toward funding. I can get you started
> right now — want me to send the link?"

## Objection handling (fast)
- **"Is this credit repair?"** → "No — it's credit **guidance**. I coach you through
  what lenders want; you stay in control. No upfront repair fees."
- **"Will you guarantee my score/funding?"** → "I can't promise numbers — anyone who
  does is lying. What I promise is a real plan and the work. Most people just need
  the right steps."
- **"$97?"** → "Less than dinner out, and it's the entry to the whole system +
  funnels working for you. Cancel anytime. The readiness check was free — this is
  where the work starts."
- **"Let me think."** → "Totally. The free check doesn't expire — but the plan only
  works if we start. Want to begin with the $97 and see week one?"

## Close → paperwork → paid (one command)
When they say yes, generate the paperwork instantly:
```
bash scripts/new-deal.sh "Client Name" 97 "$97/mo membership — credit guidance" 97
```
→ engagement + consent + intake + **deposit invoice**, filled. **Deposit = work starts.**
For bigger packages, use the right `OFFER-STACK` band amount.

## Daily rhythm for the team (so they self-run)
1. `moe-brief` — morning: ladder + compliance + today's actions
2. `moe-hot` — who to call first (replied → booked → rental→member → etc.)
3. Work the scripts above; send the free taste to every new lead
4. `new-deal` on every yes → deposit
5. Repeat. The Owner doesn't work the leads — the team does.

## Compliance (non-negotiable — protects everyone)
- **"Credit guidance," never "credit repair."**
- **No guaranteed** scores, approvals, or amounts. "Subject to lender approval."
- **No upfront repair fees** (CROA). Honor **STOP** instantly. Text **8am–9pm** local.
- **AI disclosure** first if an AI persona is messaging (`deal-kit/digitization/`).
