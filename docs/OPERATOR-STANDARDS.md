# Operator Standards — what it means to be solid here

> The bar for everyone who works with TMMT / AIXMOS. You don't have to enforce it
> by hand — the standard does the work. The right people rise to it; the wrong
> people remove themselves. This is the boss, in writing.

---

## 0. The deal
You're here to **help TMMT and AIXMOS grow so it can help everyone** — not just
yourself. You accept the mission, or you're not on the team. Simple.

## 1. Take work OFF the boss's plate (don't add to it)
A solid operator makes the owner's life **lighter**, never heavier.
- **Own your lane.** What you're given is yours to handle, start to finish.
- **Solve before you escalate.** Try first. Look it up. Figure it out. *Then* ask —
  and ask with a recommendation, not just a problem.
- **Bring solutions, not just questions.** "Here's the issue, here's what I'd do,
  do you approve?" — that's gold. "What do I do?" with no thought — that's weight.

## 2. The phone rule — protect the boss's time (this one matters)
**Do NOT blow up the boss's phone.** Most things are NOT emergencies.

| 🔴 RED — call/text NOW | 🟡 YELLOW — message (batched), reply within the day | 🟢 GREEN — DON'T message |
|---|---|---|
| Money lost or at risk | A decision only the boss can make | Routine questions |
| Security / data breach | A blocker you genuinely can't solve | Status updates |
| Client emergency, system down | Something time-sensitive but not on fire | Anything you can figure out |
| Legal / safety issue | | Anything that can wait for your daily update |

**Before you message the boss, ask yourself 4 things:**
1. Did I try to solve it myself first?
2. Is it truly 🔴 — or can it wait for my daily update?
3. Do I have a recommendation, or just a question?
4. Can I **batch** it with other items instead of pinging him one-by-one?

If it's not 🔴, it goes in your **one clean daily update** — not a stream of pings.

## 3. Integrity — non-negotiable
- **Tell the truth. Always.** A mistake told honestly is fine. A lie is the end.
- **Do what you say.** Your word is your work. If you can't deliver, say so early.
- **No working only for yourself.** If you're here just to take, you're in the wrong place.

## 4. Accountability — own it
- Own your outcomes — the wins **and** the misses. No blame, no excuses.
- Report clean: what got done, what's stuck, what you need. Short. Honest. Daily.
- One step at a time, every day. Consistency beats intensity.

## 5. How you GROW here (this is the upside)
Solid operators **rise**: more trust → more scope → more responsibility → more
earning. The people who take work off the boss's plate become the people he builds
the empire with. That's the path. It's open to anyone who actually walks it.

## 6. What gets you removed
- Lying to the owner. · Working only for yourself. · Ignoring the mission.
- Blowing up the phone with non-emergencies after being shown this.
- Adding weight instead of taking it off.

No drama, no long conversations — the standard is clear, and it decides.

---

> **The bottom line:** a good employee is someone the boss can hand a thing to,
> stop thinking about, and trust it's handled — honestly, on time, mission-first.
> Be that, and there's no ceiling here.

_Everyone agrees to this at onboarding. It's the bar. It protects the team, the
clients, and the owner. For the people, by the people — but only the people who
actually mean it. 👑_
