# 📣 Marketing & Ads Kit — copy, paste, run, sell

> Dead-simple, copy-paste ads + funnels for every business. Compliance baked in so
> the ads don't get banned. Open it anytime: **`ads`** (or `market`).
> Companions: `docs/MOE-LEGACY-CLOSING-KIT.md` (close the leads ads bring),
> `docs/LEARN-EARN-CHURN.md` (the $97 program), `docs/OFFER-STACK.md` (prices).

## 🎯 The simple funnel (every business uses this)
```
  AD  →  free thing (the "taste")  →  FORM  →  follow-up (moe-hot)  →  CLOSE  →  $$
```
The ad's only job: get the click to the free taste. The form captures them. The
closing kit does the rest. Keep ads simple — **one promise, one button.**

## 📈 MOE LEGACY — Credit Guidance + Funding ads
> ⚠️ **Compliance (read first):** Meta requires the **Special Ad Category: Credit**
> for these (set it in Ads Manager — no age/zip/gender targeting allowed). Say
> **"guidance," never "repair."** **No** guaranteed scores/approvals/amounts. No
> upfront-fee promises (CROA).

**Primary text (compliant):**
> 📈 Want to get *fundable*? Moe Legacy gives you a **free readiness check** — see
> exactly where you stand and the steps to get there. Real guidance, no hype. 👇

**Headline:** `Free Funding Readiness Check`  **Button:** `Get My Free Check`
**Audience:** Special Ad Category: Credit (broad, location only).
**Sends to:** `/forms/credit-funding-intake` → the free `/learn` readiness score (the taste).

**Soft retargeting (after they take the check):**
> You ran your readiness check — ready for the plan? Let's walk through your next
> steps. Book a free call. (Subject to lender approval. Guidance, not guarantees.)

## 🎬 Hooks that work (swap into any ad)
- "If [X] keeps telling you no, watch this." 
- "I was stuck too. Here's what changed it."
- "Free [thing]. No catch. Tap." 
- "POV: you finally got [result]." (video)
- "Most people don't know [simple truth]. Now you do."

Keep it: **1 promise → 1 proof → 1 button.** Short. Real. A face on camera beats a graphic.

## ▶️ Run ads in 5 steps (any platform)
1. **Pick ONE business + ONE ad above.** Don't mix.
2. **Set the free taste as the destination** (the form/readiness check).
3. **Budget small first:** $10–20/day for 3–4 days. Let it learn.
4. **Watch:** cost-per-lead. Under your target → scale. Over → swap the hook.
5. **Work every lead** with `moe-hot` + the closing scripts (`playbook`). Speed wins.

**Platforms by business:** Rentals → FB/IG local + Google. Credit/Funding → FB/IG
(Credit Special Category) + TikTok organic. $97 program → TikTok/Reels + FB.

## ✅ Compliance one-look (don't get banned / sued)
- **Credit:** "guidance" not "repair" · no guaranteed scores/approvals/$$ · Meta
  **Credit Special Ad Category** · no upfront-fee claims (CROA) · honor STOP.
- **$97 program:** no income guarantees — "system + path," results vary.
- **Rentals:** real terms, no deceptive approval claims.
- **Texting:** 8am–9pm local; STOP = remove instantly. AI persona? disclose it first.
