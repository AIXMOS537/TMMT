# GO GHOST → APPEAR AS X

> A personal privacy & identity-compartmentalization protocol.
> Everyday defaults: [`GHOST-EVERYDAY-DEFAULTS.md`](./GHOST-EVERYDAY-DEFAULTS.md).
> Companion tracker: [`FOOTPRINT-CLEANUP-TRACKER.md`](./FOOTPRINT-CLEANUP-TRACKER.md).

**Goal:** Make the legal you nearly invisible in public, and let a single
controlled identity — **X** — be the only thing discoverable, surfacing only
when *you* deploy it. Disappear, but not really: quiet by default, present on
demand.

**Two layers:**

- **GHOST** = the real you. Scrubbed, minimized, off public records, hardened.
  Nothing personal indexable.
- **X** = your one public-facing node (persona / brand / entity). Clean,
  authoritative, controlled. All inbound routes here.

> Define X before you build: it's the single name/handle the world sees (a
> personal-brand mononym, or an entity like AIXMOS/TMMT acting as your front).
> Pick one. Everything below points at it.

---

## THE LINE (read once, non-negotiable)

Pseudonymity and entity-shielding are **legal and standard** — founders, execs,
and high-net-worth people do exactly this. What this protocol is **not**: it
can't be used to defraud, evade taxes, dodge legal process, or misrepresent your
identity to a bank or government. Your real legal and financial obligations
still attach to the real you. A registered agent and virtual address are
precisely how you stay legitimately reachable for real process while your home
address stays private. **Stay in that lane and the ghost holds up under
scrutiny.**

## The six pillars

| # | Pillar | One-line job | Done when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SCRUB** | Remove what's already indexed | Searching your legal name returns only things *you* chose (i.e., X) |
| 2 | **SHIELD** | Stop new exposure at the source | Home address / personal name absent from every new public filing |
| 3 | **COMPARTMENTALIZE** | Ghost and X never touch | Personal cell + personal email + home address appear on nothing public |
| 4 | **HARDEN** | Lock the real you | Unique creds + app/hardware 2FA everywhere; SIM-swap PIN set |
| 5 | **SURFACE AS X** | One deliberate public node dominates | Searching X owns page one; all CTAs route to X |
| 6 | **RULES** | Keep it from regrowing | Quarterly re-audit on the calendar, habits hold |

### PILLAR 1 — SCRUB (baseline invisibility)

Finish the footprint cleanup so nothing personal is indexable.

- Work the **Audit Log** and **Broker Opt-Outs** tabs to zero open items.
- Stand up continuous broker removal (Incogni / DeleteMe / Optery) so relisting
  is auto-killed.
- Google "Results about you" → request deindex on anything tying
  name ↔ address ↔ phone.
- **Exit criterion:** searching your legal name returns only things *you* chose.

### PILLAR 2 — SHIELD (stop the leak at the source)

Most exposure *regenerates* from public filings. Cut it off going forward.

- Hold each business in an **entity** (LLC / holding co) — already your model;
  make it total.
- Replace your home address everywhere public with a **registered agent +
  virtual business address**. Use it on every registration, domain, license,
  filing.
- **WHOIS privacy** on every domain. Never register a domain in your personal
  name/address.
- Where appropriate, title assets (vehicles, property, high-value gear) through
  an entity or trust so your name isn't on the public record — confirm structure
  with an attorney/CPA.
- Bills, subscriptions, deliveries → business identity + virtual address.

### PILLAR 3 — COMPARTMENTALIZE (never cross the streams)

Separate channels so the Ghost and X never touch.

- **Email:** root identity account (locked, 2FA) → only used to control
  everything, never given out. Per-context **email aliases** (masking service)
  for every signup. X gets its own public address.
- **Phone:** personal cell is sacred and private. Public/business contact =
  **VoIP / business line(s)**. X has its own number.
- **Mail:** virtual mailbox / PO box for anything that would otherwise expose a
  physical address.
- **Cards:** virtual card numbers per vendor where possible, so purchases don't
  stitch your identity back together.
- **Rule:** personal cell + personal email + home address *never* appear on
  anything public. Ever.

### PILLAR 4 — HARDEN (lock the real you)

The Ghost only stays a ghost if the core is unbreakable.

- Single vault (Dashlane ✓) — unique credentials everywhere, nothing reused.
- 2FA on everything; prefer an authenticator / passkeys over SMS.
- **Carrier port-out / SIM-swap PIN** set with your mobile provider (closes the
  #1 takeover vector).
- Root identity email = hardware-key or app 2FA, recovery info clean, never used
  to sign up for anything.
- Quarterly: re-pull your data brokers, re-search your name, re-lock.

### PILLAR 5 — SURFACE AS X (the only thing that appears)

> **X is defined: X = AIXMOS** at `allinonemanagementsolutions.com`. Full spec:
> [`X-NODE-DEFINITION.md`](./X-NODE-DEFINITION.md).

Build one deliberate public node and make it dominate.

- **One authoritative home for X**: a site/profile you own, that states exactly
  what X is and nothing personal.
- Consistent handle for X across the few platforms you actually want indexed.
- SEO the X node so it owns page one — it becomes the default (and only) result.
- All public-facing CTAs, bios, contracts-of-first-contact route to X's
  email/number/address.
- **"Appear when needed"** = X is always *ready* but quiet; you activate it for a
  deal, a launch, a press moment, then it goes still again. You control the
  spotlight.

### PILLAR 6 — RULES OF THE GHOST (the discipline)

The architecture fails on habit, not tech. Non-negotiables:

1. Default to the **entity**, not yourself, on every form.
2. Every new account is born through an **alias**, never the root.
3. Personal contact info is **need-to-know to nobody**.
4. Re-audit **quarterly** — exposure regrows; treat it like maintenance.
5. Keep one secured map (in the vault) of which alias/number/entity maps to
   what — so *you* never lose the thread.
