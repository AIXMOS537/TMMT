## 9. ⚠️ COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS (read before ANY post goes live)
Moe Legacy touches credit → **CROA + FTC rules apply to the copy.** Non-negotiable:
- ❌ NO "guaranteed results", "we'll fix/repair/delete your credit", "raise your score X points", "remove negatives".
- ❌ NO implying payment buys a credit outcome. The $97 buys **access to a tool + education** (Learn·Earn·Churn) — NOT a credit result.
- ✅ Frame everything as **education, guidance, software access, a demo of a tool.** That's true and safe.
- ✅ Add to bio/landing: *"Educational tools & software access. Not credit repair. Results vary. Not financial or legal advice."*
- ✅ Any on-camera credit claim → run past counsel first. The demo sells the GENIE (the tool), which sidesteps this entirely — keep it there.
> This is the one thing that can actually hurt X. The demo-first angle is deliberately the safe one.
