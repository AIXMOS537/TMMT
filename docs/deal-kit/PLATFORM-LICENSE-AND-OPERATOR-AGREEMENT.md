## 1. What this is
The Owner has built and owns a software platform (the **"Platform"** — AIXMOS: the
funding/credit portal, intake forms, document system, automations, agents, mesh
tooling, and all related software, configurations, and know-how). The Owner grants
the Operator a **limited license to run the Operator's own business on the
Platform.** This is a **license, not a sale.**

## 2. Ownership (this is the leverage — keep it airtight)
- **The Platform, and all intellectual property in it, is and remains the Owner's,**
  exclusively. Nothing in this agreement transfers any ownership to the Operator.
- This includes the software, source, designs, automations, prompts/agents,
  workflows, document/templates, branding system, and all improvements — even
  improvements suggested by or built for the Operator.
- The Operator receives **only the license in Section 3.** No title, no equity, no
  source code, no right to the Platform itself.

## 3. License grant (limited, revocable)
The Owner grants the Operator a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-
sublicensable, revocable** license, for the Term, to **access and use the Platform
solely to operate {{AGENCY}}** within its permitted scope.

## 4. Restrictions (what protects the Platform)
The Operator will NOT, directly or indirectly:
- copy, clone, resell, sublicense, rent, or share the Platform or access to it;
- reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract the source/automations;
- use the Platform to build, train, or operate a **competing platform**;
- remove or alter ownership/branding notices;
- exceed the licensed scope or seats without the Owner's written approval.

## 5. The Operator's data (fair to them, clean for you)
- **The Operator owns their own client/business data** (their contacts, files,
  applications). The Owner hosts and processes it to provide the Platform.
- On termination, the Operator may **export their data** (reasonable format) within
  {{EXPORT_DAYS}} days. **The Platform, engine, and automations remain the Owner's**
  and access ends.
- Biometric/likeness and personal data follow `digitization/` (consent + retention).

## 6. Payment (this is how you get paid — see PAYMENT-SCHEDULE.md)
- The Operator pays the fees in **`PAYMENT-SCHEDULE.md`** (founder terms, license
  fee, setup, and/or revenue share), on the dates stated.
- **Late/non-payment → suspension.** If payment is {{GRACE_DAYS}} days late, the
  Owner may **suspend the license and Platform access** until cured, and may
  terminate if uncured after {{CURE_DAYS}} days. Suspension is not a breach by the
  Owner.
- Fees are **non-refundable** except as required by law. Taxes are the Operator's.

## 7. Term, suspension & termination
- **Term:** {{TERM}} from the Effective date, auto-renewing for like periods unless
  either party gives {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' written notice.
- **Owner may suspend or terminate immediately** for: non-payment (per §6), breach

- Sections 2, 4, 5 (data/ownership), 6 (accrued fees), 8–12 survive termination.

- The Operator is responsible for its own licensing/registrations for its services.

automations, and pricing are the Owner's confidential information.
