## Part 1 — Person's acknowledgment
You understand and agree that:
- Your digital self is an **AI persona** — it **reflects you but is not you**, and
  it only knows what it's been given.
- It will be **clearly disclosed as AI** to anyone who interacts with it.
- It will not be presented as a live, in-person, or factual statement by the real
  you unless you separately approve that specific use.

## Part 2 — The public-facing disclosure (what end-users see/hear)
Shown/spoken at the **start** of interactions with the persona, e.g.:

> "You're speaking with an **AI persona** of {{PERSON}}, created with their consent.
> It's a digital representation — not the real person speaking live."

Placement:
- **Voice/chat:** stated/printed as the **first** line of the interaction.
- **Video/avatar:** a persistent on-screen label (e.g., "AI persona · with consent").
- **Public posts/media:** labeled as AI-generated where the platform/law requires.

- Credit context: it says **"credit guidance," never "credit repair"**; gives **no**
  score/outcome guarantees; states "subject to lender approval."
