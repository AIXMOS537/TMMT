## ⚠️ Read this first

**The word "credit repair" never appears in any creative.** Not in captions, not in voiceover, not in comments, not in DMs. The program is **credit guidance.** Why this is non-negotiable: federal CROA + state-equivalent regs make "credit repair" a regulated advertising category. Stay clean.

The hooks below are intentionally **anti-promise** — they win attention by calling out scammy claims and positioning us as the honest alternative. That's the wedge.

## 3. Hooks bank — short-form openers

12 hooks that get attention by being **honest** rather than flashy. The credit space is so full of scams that authentic voice is a differentiator.

### Authority / contrarian hooks (strongest in this category)

- **"If anyone promises you 100 points in 30 days, run."**
  *(strongest anti-scam open — the audience is exhausted by these promises; we win by naming them)*

- **"I'm not going to fix your credit. Here's what we'll actually do."**
  *(direct contrarian — pair with on-camera operator explaining the consult process)*

- **"Why I refuse to quote a score outcome — and why every honest program does the same."**
  *(authority + compliance education in one hook)*

- **"The honest version of what credit programs do."**
  *(payoff: we educate, we route to vetted vendors, we coach — no magic)*

- **"Watch this before you pay anyone to 'fix' your credit."**
  *(warning open — strong stop-scroll on TikTok)*

### Identity hooks

- **"POV: you've spent 6 months on credit YouTube and your score hasn't moved."**
  *(empathy for the DIY rabbit-hole audience — that's the exact target)*

- **"If you've been scammed by a 'credit repair' company before, this video is for you."**
  *(strong identity, painful resonance, instantly positions us as the alternative)*

### Curiosity hooks

- **"Why DIY credit hacks work for some people and not others."**
  *(payoff: profile complexity — not every issue is a dispute issue, which is why guidance matters)*

- **"Three things real credit guidance does that no YouTube video can do for you."**
  *(payoff: vetted-vendor routing, personalized plan, weekly check-ins)*

- **"The reason most credit work fails has nothing to do with the bureaus."**
  *(payoff: most people don't follow through — the weekly check-in is the differentiator)*

### Reframe hooks

- **"Credit isn't broken. Most people's plan is."**
  *(reframe the conversation from "fix" to "plan" — supports the guidance language)*

- **"Stop guessing at your credit. Start with a real plan."**
  *(direct imperative, simple call-to-action)*

## 4. Comment & DM reply stock

Use these. Free-writing replies is the #1 way reps get the company in trouble in this category.

**"So you're a credit repair company?"**
> We're a credit guidance program — different category, different rules. We educate, coach, and connect members to vetted partners. We don't promise specific score outcomes. Anyone who does is selling you something we wouldn't.

**"How many points will my score go up?"**
> We don't quote score outcomes. Anyone who does is either lying or about to get a CFPB letter. What we will do is walk you through your profile during the consult, tell you what's realistically movable, and show you the work as it happens.

**"How long does it take?"**
> Depends on your starting profile and scope of work. The guidance program itself runs to a checklist — when the checklist is done, we evaluate together. Some members get to where they want in 60 days, some 6+ months, some never qualify for the next step and we tell them that straight.

**"How much does it cost?"**
> $500–$1,000 depending on which tier fits your profile. The tier is set during the consult, with owner approval on higher-tier placement. You have to be an active AIXMOS member ($97/mo) to access the program.

**"Why does it cost that much?"**
> Because we use real vendors for bureau-facing work, your guide spends real time on your case, and we cap how many members each operator carries so nobody becomes a number. The YouTube version exists — it costs your time and a lot of guessing.

**"Can I just do this myself?"**
> Yes. We tell members that on the consult. If you have the time, patience, and stomach for bureau response cycles, you can absolutely do it solo. We're for people who want a guided path with someone who's done it many times.

**"What if it doesn't work?"**
> "Work" gets defined up front in the written plan — concrete deliverables, not a score promise. If we don't deliver what's in the plan, that's a refund conversation under our owner-approval refund policy. If your score doesn't move as much as you hoped despite us delivering, that's the reality of credit work, and we'll have told you up front.

**"Why is this members only?"**
> Because the program works best when there's a relationship in place. The $97/month membership is how you qualify for guidance — it filters out tire-kickers and means we know who we're working with.

## 5. Compliance reminder (creative-specific)

This is the most regulation-sensitive program we run. Read it again.

**Never in any creative:**
- ❌ The phrase "credit repair" — anywhere, in any caption, in any comment
- ❌ "Guaranteed score increase," "guaranteed deletion," "X points in Y days"
- ❌ "We have a bureau contact" / "we know someone inside"
- ❌ "Risk-free" / "100% effective" / "you can't lose"
- ❌ Before-and-after screenshots of credit scores
- ❌ Naming specific tradelines or accounts in customer-facing creative

**Always:**
- ✅ The word **guidance** — every time, in every form
- ✅ State the membership prerequisite ($97/month AIXMOS membership)
- ✅ State the pricing band ($500–$1,000) honestly
- ✅ Lead with anti-promise positioning — that's our differentiator

**Anti-pattern to watch for in creator/UGC content:**
A creator says on-camera, "they raised my score by X points." If we paid for or boosted that content, that's a problem. Don't run UGC ads that quote score outcomes — even from real customers — without legal review. The cleanest UGC content is **process-focused** ("here's what the consult was like"), not **outcome-focused** ("I went from X to Y").

Reg context: federal CROA + state analogs regulate credit-improvement advertising. Our positioning as a member-gated guidance program is structurally protective — don't blow it with a sloppy caption.
