## ⚠️ Read this first

This program is the **most position-sensitive** of the three. We are a **referrer**, not a lender, not a broker-of-record. Every creative should reflect that — we don't quote amounts, we don't quote rates, we don't promise approval. What we sell is the **quality of the introduction**.

Strongest creative angles for this category:
- **Anti-stacking** ("stop applying to ten lenders at once")
- **Process** ("here's the package they actually want to see")
- **Proof-of-system** (how our funnel produces clean files)

Drop-in lines for captions, bios, ad headlines, end-card text.

- You've built a credit profile, you've shown payment discipline, you've got an entity. Now let's get you funded.

- Business funding isn't about luck. It's about the package. We build the package, you keep the capital.
- We don't fund you. We make the introduction that actually closes.

## 3. Hooks bank — short-form openers

12 hooks. The credit-impaired entrepreneur audience is bombarded by MCA spam — clean, anti-spam positioning wins.

### Contrarian / anti-MCA hooks (strongest in this category)

- **"Stop stacking applications. Start stacking your story."**
  *(direct imperative — the audience has been told by every broker to "apply everywhere")*

- **"Why one warm intro beats ten cold applications. Every time."**
  *(authority — pair with quick math on credit-pull damage from stacking)*

- **"Why MCA brokers don't want you to see this."**
  *(curiosity + contrarian — payoff: we don't take cuts off the funded amount)*

- **"Real talk: 'no upfront fee' isn't a feature, it's the standard. Here's what actually matters."**
  *(authority — payoff: the package and the partner fit matter more than the fee structure)*

### Process / proof hooks

- **"The 5 documents that decide whether you get funded."**
  *(payoff list: bank statements, ID, credit guidance summary, entity docs, operating history — fast list video)*

- **"POV: you walked into a funder's office with the package they actually wanted."**
  *(immersive POV — strong on TikTok; pair with desk B-roll, document fan-out)*

- **"The story funders actually look for in your bank statements."**
  *(curiosity — payoff: consistent deposits + healthy minimums > raw revenue)*

- **"How my customers get funded without ever submitting a cold application."**
  *(proof-of-system — emphasizes the funnel, not the individual deal)*

### Identity hooks

- **"If you've been turned down by 3+ lenders, watch this."**
  *(strong identity, pain-point open — the exact target audience)*

- **"For business owners who keep hearing 'your credit isn't there yet.'"**
  *(identity + bridge to the credit guidance funnel step)*

### Imperative hooks

- **"Stop applying. Start preparing."**
  *(simple, strong imperative — works as a 3-second hook over text-only video)*

- **"The handoff your broker never offered."**
  *(short curiosity — payoff: warm intro, documented rev-share, single-partner focus)*

## 4. Comment & DM reply stock

This is the most-likely-to-go-wrong category for free-writing replies. Stick to the stock.

**"How much can I get funded for?"**
> We don't quote approval amounts. That's the funder's call based on your real package. We've seen a wide range — what matters is the package we hand them is clean and the right partner is matched to your profile. DM for the prerequisite path.

**"What rate / APR will I get?"**
> Same answer — that's the partner's decision based on your profile. Any broker who quotes a rate before they've seen your file is making it up. What we control is the quality of the introduction.

**"Can you fund me directly?"**
> No — and we wouldn't be a good direct funder for you. Our partners have warehouse lines and licensing we don't. Our edge is in *qualifying and presenting* clients to the right partner, not in carrying the credit risk.

**"What's the upfront cost?"**
> Zero upfront for the handoff itself. The program is gated by completing AIXMOS Credit Guidance ($500–$1k, member-only) — that's the prerequisite. AIXMOS earns a rev-share from the partner on a funded deal, documented in writing before any intro is made.

**"How long does this take?"**
> Depends on where you are in the funnel. If you're not yet an AIXMOS member, that's step one ($97/mo). Credit Guidance is step two ($500–$1k, varies). The funding handoff itself is owner-gated — when your file and story are ready, we tag you `funding-prep` and the handoff begins.

**"What if the first partner says no?"**
> We reassess together. Sometimes that means more time in credit guidance, sometimes a different partner, sometimes a different funding product entirely. We don't burn through partner relationships shotgunning applications.

**"Are you a broker?"**
> No — we're a referrer. The partner is the regulated lending entity. We package, position, and introduce. Any term sheet you receive comes directly from the partner, not from us.

**"What if I have bad credit?"**
> That's not the start of this conversation — that's what the AIXMOS funnel is for. You'd come in through credit guidance first ([deck 2](../02-credit-guidance.md)), build the profile, then funding becomes a real conversation. DM us if you want the path.

## 5. Compliance reminder (creative-specific)

The line we have to hold in this category: **we are a referrer, not a lender, not a broker-of-record.** Sloppy creative blurs that line and creates regulatory exposure.

**Never in any creative:**
- ❌ Quote a funding amount ("get up to $X")
- ❌ Quote a rate, APR, or term
- ❌ Imply or state "approval guaranteed"
- ❌ Describe ourselves as a lender, broker, or "funding company" — the language is **handoff**, **referral**, **introduction**
- ❌ Show a screenshot of a funded deal amount without legal review (and never without owner sign-off)
- ❌ Suggest "no credit needed" — funding outcomes depend on the partner's underwriting, including credit

**Always:**
- ✅ Position the program as a **handoff** / **introduction**
- ✅ State the prerequisite chain honestly (member → guidance → funding-prep)
- ✅ Make it clear the partner is the regulated party who issues terms

**Channel-specific notes:**
- **TikTok / Reels:** the algorithm rewards bold claims — resist. Process and identity hooks outperform amount claims in our funnel because they bring qualified leads, not amount-shoppers.
- **Meta ads:** Meta's policy on financial services creative is strict and inconsistently enforced. Submit creative to ads review with the longest plain-text version of what we offer; abbreviated versions trigger more rejections.
- **Snap:** identity hooks ("if you've been declined…") tend to clear review faster than amount-promise hooks.

Reg context: commercial lending and merchant cash advances are regulated differently by state. Our role as **referrer** keeps us out of those licensing regimes — but only if our creatives don't accidentally hold us out as a lender or broker-of-record.
