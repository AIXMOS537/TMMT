## 4. Comment & DM reply stock

Pre-approved replies to the most common comments. **Use these. Don't free-write.**

**"Is this a scam?"**
> Real business — we run a fleet, have an office, and publish every term in writing. The agreement is a lease-purchase that's regulated in your state. DM us, we'll send you the term sheet to read before any conversation.

**"What credit score do I need?"**
> No credit minimum on this program. Income is what qualifies you ($2,800/mo gross for Standard tier). Paystubs, bank statements, or platform earnings all count.

**"How much down?"**
> Standard tier is $3,750 down. We offer a split-pay option so you can put $1,875 at signing and $1,875 within 30 days if you need to spread it.

**"What if I miss a payment?"**
> Published grace period and late fee schedule — we work with people who communicate. Stop communicating, stop paying, the car gets recovered, same as any auto loan. Difference is we want you to finish.

**"Can I pay it off early?"**
> Yes. Zero prepayment penalty. Title transfers within 14 days of your final payment.

**"What states are you in?"**
> DM us your state and we'll confirm — we're not yet in every state and we won't pitch the program in one where we're not approved to operate.

**"Where's the catch?"**
> No catch — but here's what some people consider one: TMMT holds the title until your final payment, and we require GPS on the vehicle during the term. Both standard for lease-purchase, both there to protect the program for everyone in it.

## 5. Compliance reminder (creative-specific)

Read before scheduling any post.

- ❌ Never name a specific vehicle in an ad unless it's actually in current TMMT inventory
- ❌ Never quote an insurance price (insurance is a separate add-on; pricing varies per driver)
- ❌ Never call the program "financing" or "a loan" — it's a **lease-purchase**
- ❌ Never imply "no risk" — the customer assumes payment obligations; we say so in plain English
- ❌ Never promise approval — "you qualify if you can show X, Y, Z" is fine; "you're approved" is not
- ✅ Always show the down payment, weekly/monthly payment, and term length together — never one of the three alone
- ✅ Always end with the CTA from the deck's section 12: **apply at allinonemanagementsolutions.com**

State regulations on lease-purchase / rent-to-own vehicles vary. Owner approval required before geotargeting a new state.
