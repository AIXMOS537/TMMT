## 2. Problem

Most working people in this country are locked out of car ownership by two doors:

- **The bank door** — needs a 650+ score, two years of credit history, and a clean report. If you've had a rough patch, you're out.
- **The buy-here-pay-here door** — high rates, beat-up cars, the dealer holds the title forever, and one missed payment and the car's gone with everything you've put in.

What people actually need: a real car, a real path to ownership, and a lender that judges them by their income — not by something that happened on their credit report three years ago.

---

## 3. Solution

TMMT Lease-to-Own. Three tiers. One promise: **every payment builds equity in your car**, and at the end of the term you own it — title and all — for $1.

- **No credit minimum.** Income is what matters.
- **Maintenance and GPS included** so the car keeps running and you keep moving.
- **Split-pay option on the down payment** — half at signing, half within 30 days — so you can start sooner.
- **Backed by the AIXMOS funnel.** If you want to improve your credit and build a business while you're paying off your car, we have a path for that. (Decks 2 and 3.)

## 4. Who it's for (qualifying criteria)

You qualify for Lease-to-Own if you can show all three:

1. **Verifiable income** at the tier floor (see section 7) — paystubs, bank statements, or platform earnings (Uber / Lyft / DoorDash / Instacart all count)
2. **Valid driver's license** — state-issued, not expired, not suspended
3. **Proof of address** — utility bill, lease, or government mail dated within 60 days

Insurance is required before you drive off, but you can bring your own policy **or** add the TMMT bundle as an extra line item on your payment.

**You do NOT need:**
- A FICO score
- A cosigner
- A credit-card history
- Two years of W-2s
- Anything from your past credit report

GHL tag at qualification: `l2o-qualified`

Six steps, start to title:

1. **Apply online** at [allinonemanagementsolutions.com](https://allinonemanagementsolutions.com) — 5 minutes, no hard credit pull

## 10. Top 5 objections + compliant answers

**"Is this a scam? Drive home today with no credit check?"**
> Not a scam — and that's exactly why we publish every term in writing. You're signing a lease-purchase agreement, the car is in TMMT's name until your final payment, and the law in your state regulates how that works. Read the contract. Ask any question. We're a real business with a fleet, an office, and a software stack.

**"What if I miss a payment?"**
> We have a published grace period and a late fee schedule. We work with people who communicate. If you stop communicating and stop paying, the vehicle gets recovered — that's the same as any auto loan. The difference is we don't try to trap you; we'd rather have a paying customer than a recovered car.

**"What's the actual total I'll pay?"**
> Down payment + (weekly or monthly payment × term) + $1 buyout + your insurance choice. Run the math up front; we'll do it with you. No balloon at the end, no surprise fees.

**"My credit's terrible — does that change my rate?"**
> Your credit doesn't change anything here. The tier you qualify for is set by your income, not your FICO. That's the whole point.

**"Can I pay it off early?"**
> Yes. No prepayment penalty. Pay it off whenever, title transfers within 14 days.

## 13. Compliance footer (deck-specific)

- **Never** promise a specific vehicle that isn't in current TMMT fleet inventory
- **Never** verbally adjust the published down payment, payment, or term without owner sign-off — GHL opportunity note required
- **Never** describe this as "financing" in a way that implies a bank loan — it's a **lease-purchase**
- **Never** quote insurance prices — insurance is a separate line item and depends on the customer's driver profile and carrier
- **Always** read the customer the term length and total payment count before they sign
- **Always** document the down-payment plan (single vs. split-pay) in the contract

State regulations on lease-purchase / rent-to-own vehicles vary. Owner approval is required before pitching this program in any new state not already in TMMT's active service area.
