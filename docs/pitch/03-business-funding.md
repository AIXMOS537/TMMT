## 2. Problem

Most small-business owners apply for funding the wrong way:

- They walk into a bank cold with no story, no documentation discipline, and a credit profile that's not yet been positioned for a business application
- They get bounced around predatory MCA brokers who shop their data to ten desks at once, tank their underwriting, and skim 15% off the top
- They get turned down, then get desperate, then take a stacked-position cash advance that strangles cash flow within 90 days

The thing those three paths have in common: **no operating story**. The applicant shows up as a stranger.

---

## 3. Solution

AIXMOS Business Funding Handoff. We don't fund you ourselves. We do something better:

- **We build the package.** Bank statements, ID, entity docs, credit guidance summary, TMMT rental history (if applicable) — assembled before any lender sees you.
- **We make the warm introduction.** One partner at a time, hand-picked for fit, with the package and the story already in their inbox.
- **We share the rev.** When a deal funds, the partner pays a documented share, and the operator/AIXMOS split is documented in GHL before any external commit.

You walk into the partner conversation already vetted, already documented, and already credentialed by us. That's worth a lot more than another cold application.

---

## 4. Who it's for (qualifying criteria)

This program is **gated**. We pitch it when (and only when):

1. **`credit-guidance-active` tag** in GHL — or completed guidance checklist
2. **Owner has tagged you `funding-prep`** — this is a deliberate, manual decision, not an automatic graduation
3. **You have an entity** — LLC (or corp); if not, AIXMOS can help you form one before we route
4. **You have a verifiable revenue or cash-flow story** — paystubs alone are not a business funding story; we need business bank statements, TMMT rental history, or platform earnings showing operational discipline

GHL stage at entry: `Funding Prep`

What disqualifies you (at least temporarily):
- ❌ No business entity yet (we'll help you form one first)
- ❌ Active collections case
- ❌ Bankruptcy in the last 12 months
- ❌ Refusal to share basic documentation
- ❌ Owner judgment that the profile or story isn't ready

## 5. How it works

Anchored in [`docs/sops/FUNDING-HANDOFF-SOP.md`](../sops/FUNDING-HANDOFF-SOP.md):

1. **Handoff package assembly** — we collect the documents listed in section 6 below
2. **Profile narrative** — a one-page story we write describing your operational discipline (TMMT rental history is gold here — it shows you make payments, run a fleet, manage customers)
3. **Partner selection** — owner picks the funding partner from the approved vendor list based on your profile fit
4. **Warm intro email** — a personal intro email from our team to the partner, single CTA to book your partner call
5. **Partner call** — you and the lender / broker, with our package already in their hands
6. **Stage progression** — GHL stage moves to `Funding Prep` → `Funded / Closed Won` when the partner confirms

We do not chase. One partner, one fit. If the first fit doesn't work, we re-evaluate, we don't shotgun.

## 10. Top 5 objections + compliant answers

**"How much can I get funded for?"**
> We don't quote approval amounts. That's the funder's call based on your real package. We've seen a wide range — what matters is the package we hand them is clean, and the right partner is matched to your profile.

**"What rate will I get?"**
> Same answer — that's the partner's decision based on your profile. We won't quote rates, and any broker who does pre-application is making it up. What we control is the quality of the introduction.

**"Why don't you fund me directly?"**
> Because we'd be a worse funder for you than our partners. They have warehouse lines, regulatory licenses, and risk infrastructure we don't. Our edge is in *qualifying and presenting* members, not in carrying the credit risk ourselves.

**"What's AIXMOS's cut?"**
> Documented in GHL on every deal — you can see it. Partner pays the rev-share, not you. We don't take fees from the funded amount.

**"What if the first partner says no?"**
> Then we reassess together. Sometimes that means more time in credit guidance, sometimes it means a different partner, sometimes it means a different funding product. We don't burn through partner relationships shotgunning applications.

Deal volume is gated by owner approval, intentionally, to preserve close rate and partner trust.

## 13. Compliance footer (deck-specific)

**Never:**
- ❌ Quote a guaranteed funding amount
- ❌ Quote a guaranteed rate, APR, or term
- ❌ Promise approval — that's the partner's decision
- ❌ Collect raw credit bureau data via email (use the portal / encrypted vendor channel only)
- ❌ Charge an upfront fee outside the AIXMOS published pricing without owner sign-off
- ❌ Send the member to more than one funder at a time (no stacked submissions)
- ❌ Modify the rev-share without documenting it in GHL before commit

**Always:**
- ✅ Document the rev-share in GHL opportunity notes before any external commit
- ✅ Owner approves partner selection
- ✅ Use the TMMT rental history (when applicable) as a **proof-of-operations** story, not as collateral, unless the partner specifically requests it
- ✅ Confirm the partner's terms are received by the member in writing before they sign

State and federal regulations on broker arrangements, commercial lending, and merchant cash advances vary significantly. Our role is **referrer**, not lender, not broker-of-record. The partner is the regulated party. We protect that boundary by never quoting terms and by documenting our rev-share as a marketing/referral arrangement per partner agreement.
