## ⚠️ Language rule — read first

This program is **credit guidance**. It is never *credit repair*.

The team internally knows this as "the credit repair deck," but the word *repair* never appears in:
- Ad creative
- Landing pages
- DMs, comments, or replies
- Sales calls
- SOPs (it doesn't, and we keep it that way)

Why: "credit repair" is a regulated category under the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA) and state-equivalent laws. Companies that advertise as credit repair organizations have strict disclosure, contract, and refund obligations — and "I'll fix your credit" promises are an enforcement trigger. **Guidance** describes what we actually do: we educate, we route to approved vendors, we coach on dispute and rebuilding fundamentals. We don't promise outcomes.

If a customer says "so you're a credit repair company?", the right answer is:
> "We're a credit guidance program. We educate, coach, and connect you to vetted partners. We don't promise specific score outcomes — anyone who does is selling you something we wouldn't."

## 2. Problem

The credit-fix space is full of three kinds of operators, none of them good:

- **The "guaranteed 100 points in 30 days" scammer** — illegal, FTC bait, takes your money and disappears
- **The DIY YouTube rabbit hole** — overwhelming, contradictory, and you end up doing nothing
- **The slow attorney route** — works, but costs $3k+ and feels like climbing a mountain alone

What people need: a guided plan, real coaching, vetted partner work where it's actually needed, and someone who'll tell them the truth about what's possible and what isn't.

## 10. Top 5 objections + compliant answers

**"How many points will my score go up?"**
> We don't quote score outcomes. Anyone who does is either lying or about to get a CFPB letter. What we will do is walk you through your profile, tell you what's realistically movable, and show you the work as it happens.

**"How long until I can apply for funding?"**
> Depends on your starting profile and the scope of the work. The guidance program itself is structured around a checklist — when the checklist is done, we evaluate funding readiness honestly. Some members are there in 60 days, some in 6+ months, some never qualify and we tell them that straight.

**"Why does this cost $500–$1,000?"**
> Because we use real vendors for the bureau-facing work, your guide spends real time on your case, and we cap our member count per operator so no one becomes a number. There's a YouTube version of all of this — it costs you your time and a lot of guesswork.

**"Can't I just do this myself?"**
> Yes. We tell members that on the consult. If you have the time, the patience, and the stomach for bureau response cycles, you absolutely can. We're for people who want a guided path with someone who's done it many times.

**"What happens if it doesn't work?"**
> We define "work" up front in the written plan — concrete deliverables, not a score promise. If we don't deliver what's in the plan, that's a refund conversation under our owner-approval refund policy. If your score doesn't move as much as you hoped despite us delivering, that's the reality of credit work, and we'll have told you up front.

## 13. Compliance footer (deck-specific)

This is the most regulation-sensitive deck. Read it twice.

**Never say:**
- ❌ "Credit repair" — in any context, in any channel, to anyone, ever
- ❌ "Guaranteed score increase of X points"
- ❌ "Guaranteed deletion of [tradeline / collection / late payment]"
- ❌ "We have a bureau contact" / "We know someone at [Equifax / Experian / TransUnion]"
- ❌ "Risk-free" / "No risk" / "100% effective"
- ❌ Specific timelines to a specific score ("700 in 90 days")

**Always:**
- ✅ Use the word **guidance** — in copy, on calls, in comments
- ✅ Set expectations in writing during the consult
- ✅ Document tier placement and pricing rationale in GHL
- ✅ Route bureau-facing work to approved vendors only
- ✅ Owner approval on any refund or payment-plan exception

**Required at sign-up:**
- Member must already hold an active $97/month AIXMOS membership
- Written plan must be delivered before vendor routing
- Refund policy reference must appear in the invoice

Regulatory context: the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA) and several state analogs regulate organizations that advertise credit-improvement services. Operating as a guidance program with a member-prerequisite and explicit no-guarantee positioning is intentional and protective. **Do not freelance language that could be construed as a credit-repair claim — even in a TikTok comment, even in a DM, even off-camera.**
