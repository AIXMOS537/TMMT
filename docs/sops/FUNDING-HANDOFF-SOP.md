# Business funding handoff SOP

## When to start

- Contact has tag `credit-guidance-active` or completed credit guidance checklist  
- Owner tags `funding-prep` in GHL  

## Handoff package (collect before partner intro)

- Last 3 months business bank statements (if applicable)  
- ID + proof of address  
- Credit guidance summary (no raw bureau data in email)  
- Business entity docs (LLC if formed via AIXMOS)  
- TMMT rental history (if they were a customer — proves cash flow story)  

## Partner routing

1. Owner selects funding partner from approved vendor list  
2. Warm intro email — CHUMMO voice, single CTA to book partner call  
3. GHL stage → **Funding Prep** → **Funded / Closed Won** when partner confirms  

## Revenue share

Document operator/partner split in GHL opportunity notes — owner approves before external commit.

## Compliance

- No guaranteed approval amounts or rates  
- No upfront fees outside published AIXMOS pricing without owner sign-off  

## TMMT advantage narrative (for funders)

Use rental business as **proof of operations discipline** — fleet, collections, repeat customers — not as collateral unless partner requests it.
