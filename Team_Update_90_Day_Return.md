## 3) AI Assist by Department — The Cheat Sheet

*(Share this as a follow-up message or printed handout after the Meet. Tools referenced: ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI assistant the team prefers.)*

### 🚗 Rentals / Front Desk / Customer Service

**What AI can do for you:**

- **Draft customer replies in seconds** — paste a customer's message, ask AI to "write a polite, professional reply explaining [the situation]." Great for refund questions, complaints, rental extensions, and damage disputes.
- **Translate on the fly** — Spanish, Arabic, anything. Paste the message, ask for the translation, send it.
- **Phone script generator** — "Give me a 3-line script to call a customer whose return is overdue." No more freezing on the call.
- **Summarize long email threads** — paste 20 emails of back-and-forth, get a 4-line summary of what's actually been agreed.
- **Reservation confirmation rewrites** — make confirmation messages sound friendlier, clearer, more on-brand.
- **Difficult customer prep** — describe the situation, ask AI for 3 ways to de-escalate before you pick up the phone.

**Quick prompt to copy:**

> "I'm a front desk agent at a vehicle rental company. A customer is upset because [situation]. Write a calm, professional reply that acknowledges their frustration, explains [policy/fact], and offers [resolution]."

---

### 🔧 Detailing / Maintenance / Shop

**What AI can do for you:**

- **Translate maintenance logs into plain English** — for owner/customer reports, insurance claims, or internal records.
- **Write up damage reports** — describe what you saw on the vehicle, AI structures it into a clean inspection note.
- **Parts and supplies research** — "What's a reliable replacement part for [model/year]? Give me 3 options with price ranges." Saves Google rabbit holes.
- **Cleaning / detailing SOPs** — generate step-by-step checklists for new hires. "Write me a 15-step interior detailing checklist for a returning rental."
- **Diagnose from symptoms** — describe a noise, smell, warning light — get a list of likely causes before you tear into it.
- **Shift handoff notes** — dump bullet points into AI, get a clean handoff message for the next shift.

**Quick prompt to copy:**

> "I'm a vehicle detailer/mechanic. Take these rough notes and turn them into a clean, professional maintenance log entry for our records: [paste your notes]"

---

### 🚙 Fleet / Logistics / Drivers

**What AI can do for you:**

- **Route planning** — "I need to pick up vehicles from [A], [B], [C] and return them to the shop. What's the most efficient order?"
- **Inspection checklist generator** — pre-trip, post-trip, return inspections — generate the right checklist for any vehicle type in seconds.
- **Incident reports** — describe what happened (accident, breakdown, customer issue), get a clean, factual incident report.
- **Mileage / fuel logs cleanup** — paste messy notes, get a clean structured log.
- **Driver communication scripts** — "I need to call a customer whose vehicle is being repossessed. Give me a professional, firm but respectful script."
- **Weather / road condition summaries** — paste a weather forecast, ask "what should drivers be aware of today?"

**Quick prompt to copy:**

> "I'm a fleet driver/logistics coordinator. I just did [task / inspection / pickup]. Turn this into a clean report I can drop into the system: [paste rough notes]"

---

### 📊 Sales / Marketing / Admin / Accounting

**What AI can do for you:**

**Sales:**

- Draft follow-up emails to leads ("write a follow-up to a customer who asked about a 7-day rental last week").
- Build sales scripts for upsells (insurance, extended rentals, premium vehicles).
- Summarize lead lists — paste a CSV, ask "which of these are most likely to convert based on [criteria]?"

**Marketing:**

- Write Instagram / Facebook / TikTok captions for fleet photos.
- Generate 30 days of social post ideas in one prompt.
- Draft Google review responses (both 5-star and the 1-star ones).
- Write promotional flyers, email blasts, and SMS campaigns.

**Admin:**

- Draft policy documents, employee memos, and HR notices.
- Turn meeting notes into action-item lists.
- Build SOPs (standard operating procedures) for any recurring task — "write me an SOP for onboarding a new driver."
- Calendar / scheduling logic — "we have 12 vehicles and 4 drivers, here's the demand — give me a weekly schedule."

**Accounting / Bookkeeping:**

- Categorize transactions — paste a list of expenses, ask AI to label them (fuel, maintenance, marketing, payroll, etc.).
- Explain accounting concepts in plain English so you can talk to the CPA confidently.
- Draft invoice reminders / collection letters in different tones (gentle, firm, final notice).
- Reconciliation checklists — generate a month-end close checklist tailored to a rental business.

**Quick prompt to copy:**

> "I work in [sales / marketing / admin / accounting] at a vehicle rental company called TMMT Rentals. I need help with [specific task]. Here's the raw info: [paste]. Give me a clean, professional output I can use today."

## 4) Ground Rules for AI Use (One Line Each)

- **Never paste a customer's full credit card, SSN, or driver's license number into AI.** Names, emails, and rental details are fine — sensitive ID/financial data is not.
- **AI drafts — humans approve.** Always read what it gives you before sending it to a customer.
- **If you're unsure, ask in the team chat.** Someone else has probably figured it out already.
- **Tell me what's working and what's not.** That's how the new system gets better.
