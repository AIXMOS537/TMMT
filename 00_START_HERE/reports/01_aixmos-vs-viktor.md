# Report 1 — AIXMOS vs. Viktor (competitive + GTM)

Full report delivered as an artifact in the planning conversation. Key decisions
that drive this build (do not relitigate in code):

- Position AIXMOS as a VERTICAL product for Turo/independent fleet operators, not a
  horizontal "AI employee." Do not try to out-feature Viktor.
- Three structural wins to lead with: local inference + data sovereignty (NAS/Ollama),
  owner-approval gate, pay-once-own-forever economics.
- Viktor leads on GTM, funding ($75M), Slack-native UX, 3,200+ integrations, onboarding.
  Close the gaps that matter to OUR niche only: easy setup, fleet-economics dashboard,
  the ~15 connectors fleet operators actually use, a sharp brand line.
- Pricing decision: one-time license + local Ollama default + BYO-key for cloud calls.
  Never absorb uncapped inference cost against a one-time fee.
- Beachhead: fleet operators first (use TMMT's own numbers as the case study), then widen.
