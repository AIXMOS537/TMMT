# Report 2 — Credit Repair + Business Funding + Community Capital Vertical

Full report delivered as an artifact in the planning conversation. Build-critical points:

- Funding ladder: personal credit repair (CROA) -> business credit (EIN/DUNS/net-30)
  -> business funding (cards/LOC/SBA/equipment; MCA/RBF gated) -> community capital
  (CDFI + Virginia SSBCI partnerships, NOT a self-run fund) -> Credit-to-Keys ownership.
- Legal stack: CROA (no advance fee, written contract, separate disclosure, 3-day cancel,
  no false statements) + Virginia Credit Services Businesses Act (VDACS registration +
  surety bond) + TSR advance-fee ban. MCA/RBF = Virginia SCC sales-based financing broker
  registration + 9-item disclosure. Pooled fund = securities (Howey/'40 Act) -> avoid.
- Operator economics: Tier 1 refer ($50-150/credit referral; 1-3% or 15-25% of origination
  on funding); Tier 2 certified (40-60% rev share); Tier 3 capital partner ($50K, bilateral).
  Pay ONLY on real client services, never recruitment.
- Community protection: conduct-based covenant, uniform enforcement, never identity/protected
  class, no gag clauses, watch joint-employer overreach.
- Never take custody of client funds. PII on NAS tier. CPNs/rented tradelines permanently banned.
- Phasing: legal foundation -> pilot credit+business-credit -> funding desk -> CDFI/SSBCI
  community capital -> capital-partner tier -> multi-state only after Virginia proven.
