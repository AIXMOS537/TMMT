# Credit Guidance SOP (AIXMOS)

**Positioning:** Credit **guidance** only — not credit repair. No guaranteed score or deletion outcomes.

## Eligibility

- Active `member-97` tag in GHL  
- Completed AIXMOS intake ([`/apply`](/apply) or GHL form)  
- Owner or executive VA approval for high-ticket send  

## Steps

1. **Consult (30 min)** — review goals, timeline, and documents needed  
2. **Package** — select tier ($500–$1K per [`project_aixmos_chummo`](../../../AIX-Command-Center/docs/AIXMOS/project_aixmos_chummo.md) pricing)  
3. **Payment** — GHL invoice or checkout; tag `credit-guidance-active`  
4. **Vendor routing** — assign guidance partner from approved vendor list  
5. **Weekly check-in** — operator or VA logs touchpoint in GHL  
6. **Completion** — deliverable checklist signed; tag `funding-prep` if funding is next  

## GHL stages

`Member $97` → `Credit Consult Booked` → `Credit Guidance Active` → `Funding Prep`

## Owner approval required

- Any promise about score ranges or timelines  
- Refunds or payment plan exceptions  
- Sharing client PII outside approved vendors  

## TMMT cross-sell rule

Do not offer credit guidance during active rental dispute or open collections case.
