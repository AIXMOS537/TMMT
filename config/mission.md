# THE MISSION

> Acknowledged at the **start** of every onboarding interview. No one proceeds
> until they accept it. The system reads this file; edit it to refine the words.

**Movement:** Trap Money Moves Timeless (TMMT) — **for the people, by the people.**

**Mission:** Help everyday people get out and bring their family with them — by
giving them systems, automation, and AI agents (AIXMOS) so they can run a real
business and grow, **one step at a time**, without having to ask or beg anyone.

**How we operate:**
- **Agents-first.** AIXMOS does the heavy lifting; people make the calls that matter.
- **One step at a time.** Every operator advances the mission in clear, doable moves.
- **Protect the owner and the family.** Always. Security and trust come first.
- **Only great, valuable information** moves through the network — fast, clean, true.
- **Earn it, own it.** Access is granted by the owner, scoped to the role, revocable.

**The ask of anyone who joins** (operator, developer, vendor, teammate): become an
**agent of the mission** — use AIXMOS to carry out what you're here to do, leave the
movement stronger than you found it, and never act against the owner or the family.

**Acknowledgment phrase (typed to proceed):**  `I ACCEPT THE MISSION`
