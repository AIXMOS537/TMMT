## How the three decks fit the AIXMOS-TMMT funnel

These programs are sequenced, not parallel. Each one earns the right to pitch the next.

```
Lease-to-Own customer (TMMT)
        │
        │  positive payment history, 60+ days in
        ▼
 $97/mo AIXMOS Membership
        │
        │  member-97 tag + intake complete
        ▼
 Credit Guidance ($500–$1k)
        │
        │  guidance complete, owner tags funding-prep
        ▼
 Business Funding handoff (partner)
```

Source of truth for the funnel: [`docs/AIXMOS-TMMT-FUNNEL.md`](../AIXMOS-TMMT-FUNNEL.md).

**Do not skip steps.** The funnel is what makes our credit-guidance and funding pitches work — without a proven payment history on the lease, those upsells have no story.

## How to use these decks

Each deck has the same 13-section structure so the team can jump to the section they need:

1. One-line positioning
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Who it's for (qualifying criteria)
5. How it works (the step-by-step)
6. What you get (deliverables)
7. Pricing & terms
8. The TMMT × AIXMOS advantage
9. Where it leads (cross-sell into the next deck)
10. Top 5 objections + compliant answers
11. Audience-specific framing blocks (customer / sales rep / referral partner / investor)
12. Call to action — exact GHL tag, exact next step
13. Compliance footer (deck-specific)

**Pull the section you need for the channel you're in:**

- Customer DM, ad creative, landing page → sections 1, 2, 3, 7, 12
- Sales rep coaching → sections 4, 5, 10, 13
- Referral partner pitch → sections 8, 11 (referral block), 12
- Investor / capital partner update → sections 8, 9, 11 (investor block)

## Universal Compliance Footer

These rules apply to every channel, every audience, every deck. There are no exceptions.

### What we never say

- ❌ "Credit repair" — we say **credit guidance**
- ❌ "Guaranteed approval" — for anything (lease, credit, funding)
- ❌ "We'll fix your credit score" / "Raise your score by X points"
- ❌ "Guaranteed funding amount" or "guaranteed rate"
- ❌ "No risk" / "Risk-free" on any financial product
- ❌ Specific score outcomes ("get to 700 in 90 days," etc.)
- ❌ Promises about deletions from credit reports

### What we always do

- ✅ Use the program's published pricing — no off-menu discounts without owner sign-off
- ✅ Apply the correct GHL tag for the funnel stage (see each deck's section 12)
- ✅ Route client data only through approved vendors
- ✅ Document any revenue share in GHL opportunity notes before external commit
- ✅ Owner approval required for: refunds, payment-plan exceptions, score-range claims, sharing PII outside approved vendors

### Why this matters

We operate in regulated lanes. The FTC, CFPB, and state attorneys general actively enforce against companies that promise specific credit outcomes or guaranteed funding. Sloppy language — even in a TikTok comment — is the kind of thing that gets a business shut down. Our funnel works because it's real. We protect it by talking about it accurately.
