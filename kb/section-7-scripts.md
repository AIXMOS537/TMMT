> Canonical source: `OPERATIONS_BRAIN.md §7`

## BANNED WORDS — Never Draft, Never Send

❌ guarantee · approved · financed · funded · get you a car · get you a house · no risk · 100% · I promise

✅ Always use instead: coach · plan · guide · help · work with · most clients see results when · we give you a plan

---

> Quick three:
> 1. When do you need it by?
> 2. Can you put down a deposit + first week's payment today to lock it in?
> 3. Do you have a valid driver's license?
>
> [If yes to all 3:] Perfect. Our weekly is [$X]. Deposit is [$Y]. Total today: [$Z]. I'll text you a payment link — once it clears, I'll get you scheduled to pick up the car tomorrow. Sound good?
>
> [If hesitating:] I get it. To hold the car I need the deposit today, otherwise it goes to the next person calling. Want to lock it?"

## Agent Draft Rules

When drafting any outbound message on behalf of a team member:

1. Pick the correct template above based on the rep and context.
2. Fill in `[name]`, `[$X]`, `[$Y]`, `[$Z]` with real values from the lead record.
3. Do not add promises, guarantees, or outcome claims.
4. Route the draft through the **owner-approval gate** before sending — no message goes out unsupervised.
5. Tag the content for TMMT OR AIXMOS — never both on the same asset.

See `AIXMOS_OPERATING_LAYER.md §7` for the full owner-approval gate rules.
