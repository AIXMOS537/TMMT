# Red Hood — pre-compliant ad templates (credit + funding)

> Ready-to-run ad copy that is CROA/FTC-safe. These pass the compliance gate (no
> guarantees, no "+points", no advance-fee language) and carry the disclosures.
> Edit the offer, keep the disclosures. Run `check-my-ad` before posting anything new.

This is educational guidance, not legal or financial advice. Results vary and no
specific outcome is guaranteed.

---

## Ad 1 — Credit education (lead magnet)
Feeling stuck with your credit? You have the right to dispute inaccurate information
in your own credit report for free. We offer step-by-step educational guidance to
help you understand your report and your options. Book a free consult.
*Educational guidance, not legal or financial advice. Results vary; nothing is guaranteed.*

## Ad 2 — Funding readiness
Want to get your business funding-ready? We help you organize, understand lender
requirements, and build a readiness plan. We are not a lender or loan broker unless
separately licensed in your state, and this is not a guarantee of funding.
*Educational guidance, not legal or financial advice. Results vary.*

## Ad 3 — Free consult (soft CTA)
Book a free 15-minute consult. We'll walk you through how credit and funding
readiness work, in plain language — no pressure. You may cancel any agreement within
3 business days, and we do not charge for credit-repair services before they are
performed.
*Educational guidance, not legal or financial advice. Results vary; no guarantee.*

You have the right to dispute inaccurate information in your own credit report for
free. You may cancel within 3 business days at no charge.
